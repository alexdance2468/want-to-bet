How Want to Bet works

What is it?

Want to Bet is a place to back your opinions with fake money. You say something will happen, choose the odds you're willing to offer, and other people decide whether to bet against you. No real money is involved, and there's no login.

1. Type your name

On the Home page, type your name in the Your name box. This is the name shown on your offers and bets, and your browser remembers it for next time.

2. Make an offer

Write what you're betting on, for example "It will rain in Sydney on Saturday", then fill in two numbers:

  • Odds: how much you'll pay out for every 1 someone bets against you.
  • Most you'll pay out: your total limit across everyone who bets.
Example: You offer odds of 2 with a most-you'll-pay-out of 100.
  • Sam bets 10 against you. If you lose, you pay Sam 20. If you win, Sam pays you 10.
  • Up to 50 in total can be bet against you, because 50 × 2 = 100.

3. Bet against an offer

Find an offer you disagree with, type how much you want to bet (any amount up to what's left), and click Bet. Several people can each take a piece of the same offer until the limit is used up. You can't bet against your own offer.

4. Offer better odds

Think you can do better? Click Offer better odds under any bet to post your own offer on the same statement. Your odds have to be higher than the best offer still available, because higher odds pay more to the person betting. Each offer has its own limit, and the best odds are shown first.

5. Say who won

Once the outcome is known, both people in the bet go to Home, find the bet under Your bets awaiting a result, and click I won or I lost.

  • If your answers match, the result is locked in and the leaderboard updates.
  • If they don't match, the bet stays open and shows that you disagree. Either of you can change your answer until you agree.
  • Each bet is settled separately, so if two people bet on the same offer, each pair settles their own bet.

If nobody has bet on your offer yet, you can click Cancel offer instead.

The Details page

  • Odds over time: pick a bet to see a chart and table of every offer made on it, oldest to newest.
  • Head to head: pick two people to see every bet between them, who won each one, and who is ahead overall.

The leaderboard

Everyone's net winnings across settled bets. It starts at 0, goes up when you win, and can go below 0 when you lose. There's no wallet or starting balance.

Good to know

  • It runs on honesty. Nothing stops two people from both claiming they won, so it works best among people who trust each other.
  • Names aren't locked. Anyone can type any name, so use names your group recognises.
  • Use the same device. Your offers and bets are tied to the browser you made them from. If you clear your browser data or switch devices, you can no longer say who won on them.
  • It's all fake. The numbers are just for bragging rights.
