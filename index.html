How Want to Bet works

What is it?

Want to Bet is a place to back your opinions with fake money. You say something will happen, choose the odds you're willing to offer, and other people decide whether to bet against you. No real money is involved, and there's no login.

1. Pick a group

Everything on the site lives inside a group, and each group has its own bets, topics and leaderboard.

  • Everyone (public) is the default group. Anyone can see it, and it's where you land if you don't join another group.
  • Create a group under Create or join a group to get a private space. You'll get an invite link to share.
  • Join a group by opening an invite link, or by pasting a link or code into the Join box. Your groups appear in the dropdown.

The invite link is the only protection, so only share it with people you want in the group.

2. Type your name

On the Home page, type your name in the Your name box. This is the name shown on your offers and bets, and your browser remembers it for next time.

3. Make an offer

Write what you're betting on, for example "It will rain in Sydney on Saturday", then fill in:

  • Odds: how much you'll pay out for every 1 someone bets against you.
  • Most you'll pay out: your total limit across everyone who bets.
  • Topic: a category such as NRL or Work. Use Add a new topic to create one. Anyone in the group can add topics, and you can filter Home by topic.
  • Betting closes (optional): after this time nobody can bet. Leave it blank for no closing time.
Example: You offer odds of 2 with a most-you'll-pay-out of 100.
  • Sam bets 10 against you. If you lose, you pay Sam 20. If you win, Sam pays you 10.
  • Up to 50 in total can be bet against you, because 50 × 2 = 100.

4. Bet against an offer

Find an offer you disagree with, type how much you want to bet (any amount up to what's left), and click Bet. Several people can each take a piece of the same offer until the limit is used up. You can't bet against your own offer, and you can't bet once betting has closed.

5. Offer your own odds

Want to back the same statement at different odds? Click Offer your own odds under any bet to post your own offer on it. Your odds can be higher or lower than the others. All the offers on a bet are listed together with the best odds first, and the person betting chooses whichever one they like. Each offer has its own limit and shares the same closing time.

6. Say who won

Once betting has closed (or straight away if there's no closing time), the bet appears under Ready to settle. Anyone in the group can click the name of the winner, such as Alex won or John won. The result is final straight away, and the leaderboard updates.

Each bet is settled separately, so if two people bet on the same offer, each of those bets gets its own result.

If nobody has bet on your offer yet, you can click Cancel offer instead.

The Details page

  • Odds over time: pick a bet to see a chart and table of every offer made on it, oldest to newest.
  • Head to head: pick two people to see every bet between them, who won each one, and who is ahead overall.

The leaderboard

Everyone's net winnings across settled bets in the current group. It starts at 0, goes up when you win, and can go below 0 when you lose. There's no wallet or starting balance.

Good to know

  • It runs on honesty. Anyone in the group can declare a winner and the result can't be changed afterwards, so only settle a bet when you're sure of the outcome.
  • Names aren't locked. Anyone can type any name, so use names your group recognises.
  • It's all fake. The numbers are just for bragging rights.
